Loungeless.

Lounge less, do more. A field notebook from Harrison Greanias on AI, cybersecurity, telecom, identity, enterprise technology, and markets. I focus on the gap between the demo and production.

I went legally blind at 10 from Stargardt's disease, tied to the ABCA4 gene. I grew up navigating a world that wasn't built for me, which gave me dark humor, radical candor, and a habit of seeing what other people miss. Loungeless is the invitation to resist passive thinking, leave the comfortable answer, and look at how systems actually work.
